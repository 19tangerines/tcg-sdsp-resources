The San Diego Science Project (SDSP) at UC San Diego provides professional learning and curriculum support to K-12 science teachers. Our workshops are grounded in research-based practices and designed with real classroom challenges in mind to support educators and their students.

Whether you are looking to deepen your content knowledge, try new instructional approaches, or join a network of colleagues who share your commitment to bringing meaningful science to every student, there is a place for you here. We'd love to have you join us!

– The San Diego Science Project Team
