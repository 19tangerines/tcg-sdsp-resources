Summer Professional Learning Events: Science Media Literacy Workshop @ UC San Diego

Hi there, 

If you've ever had students bring up something they saw on TikTok, Youtube, or the news and weren't sure how to help them evaluate it, you're not alone.

This summer, the San Diego Science Project at UCSD is offering a new hands-on Science Media Literacy Workshop designed to help teachers navigate this challenge.

Science Media Literacy Workshop

In today's world, students encounter scientific claims everywhere, from social media to AI tools. This one-day interactive workshop equips educators with practical frameworks to help students

  • evaluate science-related claims
  • identify misinformation tactics
  • think critically about sources

You'll leave with classroom-ready materials to use right away.

The San Diego Science Project (SDSP) is a partnership between UC San Diego and the San Diego County Office of Education, serving educators across San Diego and Imperial Counties. As the regional site of the statewide California Science Project, SDSP is part of a trusted network dedicated to strengthening science education from the ground up.

SDSP co-creates programs and learning communities with a focus on equity, teacher leadership, and real-world science connections. We build bridges between the science research community and K-12 teachers, partnering with institutions like the Scripps Institution of Oceanography and the Birch Aquarium to bring cutting-edge science into classrooms.

SDSP offers professional learning experiences designed by educators, for educators. Our workshops are based on the latest science research and built around what actually works in K-12 classrooms, providing long-term, targeted professional development that resonates with teachers and their students.

Whether you are looking to refresh your practice, connect with a community of science educators, or bring new ideas back to your students, there is a place for you here. We'd love to have you join us!

– The San Diego Science Project Team
