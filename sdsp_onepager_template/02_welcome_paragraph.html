Hi there,

It's nice to meet you! We're the San Diego Science Project (SDSP), based at UC San Diego, a community providing professional learning and curriculum support to K-12 science teachers across the greater San Diego region.

Our work is grounded in research-based practices and built around the real challenges teachers face in their classrooms. We believe science learning should be inclusive, driven by sensemaking, and connected to the questions that matter most to students and their communities.

Whether you're looking to deepen your content knowledge, try new instructional approaches, or join a network of colleagues who share your commitment to bringing meaningful science to every student, there's a place for you here.
