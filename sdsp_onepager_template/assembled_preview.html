Welcome to the San Diego Science Project

Hi there,

It's nice to meet you! We're the San Diego Science Project (SDSP), a partnership between UC San Diego and the San Diego County Office of Education serving educators across both San Diego and Imperial Counties. As the regional site of the statewide California Science Project, we're part of a trusted network dedicated to strengthening science education from the ground up.

SDSP co-creates programs and learning communities to support the interests and needs of science educators throughout the region, with a focus on equity, teacher leadership, and real-world science connections. We build bridges between the science research community and K-12 teachers, partnering with institutions like the Scripps Institution of Oceanography and the Birch Aquarium to bring cutting-edge science into classrooms.

Whether you're looking to deepen your content knowledge, try new instructional approaches, or join a network of colleagues who share your commitment to bringing meaningful science to every student, there's a place for you here.

Explore more at SDSP's website, or scroll down to see what we have coming up!
